# Blue Owl Capital Case Study

#### More Fear in Private Credit?

* **Financial Statement Analysis:** Automated the extraction and standardization of 5-year historical financial data to run comparable company valuation and data integrity checks.
* **Interactive Reporting:** Synthesized raw fundamental data and quantitative risk assessments into a finalized equity research report and dynamic dashboard.

###  Files

* **`Blue Owl Notebook.ipynb`**: Loads historical stock price & is a automates the ingestion, cleaning, and standardization of 5-year Income Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flows into queryable datasets.
* **`Blue_Owl_Capital_Report.pdf`**: The finalized equity research report. 

Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Net Income are attached as well as the closing price of last 5 years.
